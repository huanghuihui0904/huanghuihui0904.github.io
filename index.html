Huihui Huang

CS PhD candidate at Singapore Management University

I am a second-year PhD candidate in the Software Analytics Research (SOAR) group at the Singapore Management University (SMU), fortunate to be advised by Prof. David Lo (ACM and IEEE Fellow). I am also grateful to be mentored by Dr. Jieke Shi. Before starting my PhD, I completed my Bachelor’s degree at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I was mentored by Prof. Yuqun Zhang.

Contact: hh.huang.2024@phdcs.smu.edu.sg or hhhuang@smu.edu.sg

Research Interests:
  • Penetration testing
  • Vulnerability Detection
  • Software Traceability
