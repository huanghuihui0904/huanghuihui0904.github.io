Huihui Huang

CS PhD candidate at Singapore Management University

I am a second-year PhD candidate in the Software Analytics Research (SOAR) group at the Singapore Management University (SMU), fortunate to be advised by Prof. David Lo. Before starting my PhD, I completed my Bachelor’s degree at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I was mentored by Prof. Yuqun Zhang.

Contact: hh.huang.2024@phdcs.smu.edu.sg or huihuihuang0904@gmail.com
